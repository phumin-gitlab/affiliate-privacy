Privacy Policy — Affiliate

Last updated: 15 October 2026

"Affiliate" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Facebook Pages it manages, to upload and schedule videos and images to those Pages and to read the Pages' own post statistics.

What data it uses

  • Access tokens for Pages the owner manages, stored privately on the owner's computer.
  • The Pages' own posts, comments and insights (views, watch time, reactions), used only to report how the Pages' content performs.

What it does not do

  • It does not offer Facebook Login to the public and does not collect personal data from other people.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Data deletion

The tool stores no data about Facebook users other than the Page owner. To request deletion of any data connected to this app, email itsgonena@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion"; it will be deleted within 30 days. You can also remove the app at any time in Facebook Settings > Business integrations.
